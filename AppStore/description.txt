Pitch Pact — Youth Soccer Match Day

Pitch Pact helps youth and recreational soccer teams plan games, manage rosters and uniforms, track availability, and keep a match-day play-by-play ledger on iPhone and Android.

Create a team and roster, schedule tournament or one-off games, record location and uniform requirements, check RSVP availability locally, and set game reminders on your device.

On game day, use the live clock and log goals, cards, substitutions, and extra events. Capture continues offline; the local ledger retains corrections and submitted entries. The official score is separate from the pending score: a goal does not become official without an opposing-side acceptance. Cross-team consensus review is planned but not yet available.

Managers running their own server can start a private broadcast and share a six-character code. Spectators can read the score, clock snapshot, labeled pending changes, and play-by-play through an app screen or browser page without creating an account. Broadcast updates require connectivity and a configured server; the public hosted service has not been deployed.

For youth privacy, rosters, guardian contacts, and availability are not included in broadcast payloads. Broadcast events use jersey numbers, not player names. Managers control whether to start or stop sharing a match.
