Pitch Pact — Run Your Season. Share the Game. Score Together.

Pitch Pact brings youth and recreational soccer teams into one place: set up your team and roster, plan tournaments and ad-hoc games, track uniform requirements and field locations, collect availability, and send match-day reminders — on iPhone and Android.

On game day, Pitch Pact is a live scorer built for the sideline. Keep the clock, tap goals, cards, subs, and play-by-play as they happen — even with zero signal in the parking lot. Undo anything; the full story is always kept.

Share the game with anyone. Broadcast mode sends the live score, clock, and key moments to a short 6-character code friends and family can follow from the app or a web page — no accounts, no sign-in, fully anonymous. Grandparents on the far sideline (or the sick teammate at home) finally see the same score at the same time.

Two teams. Two scorers. One scoreboard. Pitch Pact's team registry lets any two teams link with a shared team code, confirm fixtures, and co-officiate the match: each side's scorer logs their own play-by-play, and a goal only becomes official on the shared scoreboard once the other side accepts it. Disagreements never erase the record — every logged moment stays in the ledger.

Why families and managers love Pitch Pact:
• Team, roster, and uniform tracking built for youth soccer
• Tournaments, brackets, standings, plus casual league and one-off games
• Availability RSVPs so you know who's in before warm-ups
• Live play-by-play scoring that works fully offline
• Consensus scoring — both teams agree before the score is official
• Anonymous broadcast with a short share code (app or web), no account needed
• Works on iPhone and Android with the same logic and rules

Pitch Pact is designed for youth soccer privacy: rosters, guardian contacts, and availability stay private to your team. Broadcasts publish only the score, clock, and game events — never your team's personal data, and you can even broadcast by jersey number only.

Your data belongs to you: full export, backup, and deletion controls, built right in.
